## A. Research Protocol and Dataset

### Dataset Composition & Split Integrity

- **Source Dataset:** `civicflow_400_labeled_complaint_pairs.csv` (SHA-256: `b4653ac507f422a3b6f5ffadb291d5440eab0cb07174f2398ad72739e86a33f2`)
- **Splits Manifest:** `splits.json` (Seed: `42`)
- **Train Split:** **240** complaint pairs (60.0%)
- **Validation Split:** **80** complaint pairs (20.0%)
- **Held-Out Test Split:** **80** complaint pairs (20.0%)
- **Synthetic Challenge Set:** **150** complaint pairs (separate challenge partition)
- **Grouping Protocol:** Grouped by `incident_id` to guarantee **zero group leakage** across partition boundaries. All complaints linked to a given incident reside entirely within a single split.

### Training and Validation Rules

1. **TF-IDF Vocabulary Isolation:** The vocabulary (97 terms) and smoothed inverse document frequency (IDF) weights were fitted strictly on the **240 training pairs** (480 training texts). Out-of-vocabulary terms in validation, test, or challenge sets are completely ignored during transform.
2. **Validation-Only Threshold Selection:** Decision boundaries (duplicate threshold $\theta_{dup}$ and related threshold $\theta_{rel}$) were selected strictly on the **80 validation pairs** using an explicit uniform grid of 136 admissible pairs in $[0.10, 0.90]$ with step $0.05$ subject to $\theta_{dup} > \theta_{rel}$.
3. **Strict Split Sequencing & Isolation:** **Neither the held-out test set nor the synthetic challenge set was used for tuning or model selection.** They remain strictly withheld until the final evaluation freeze (T6) to prevent data leakage and benchmark gaming.

### Recurring Failure Modes & Observed Evidence vs. Hypotheses

1. **Lexical Paraphrase Brittleness (B1 & B4):**
   - *Directly Observed Evidence:* Pair `P0005` (true label `duplicate`) features texts *"Water is escaping onto the road from a damaged pipe"* vs. *"Continuous water leakage reported close to"*. B1 score is `0.1667` (below threshold `0.20`), predicting `independent` despite distance of only 127m.
   - *Likely Cause Hypothesis:* Surface lexical token overlap cannot bridge distinct synonymous phrasing without concept normalization or spatial conditioning.

2. **Spatial Conflation of Duplicate vs. Related (B2):**
   - *Directly Observed Evidence:* Distance-only model predicts `duplicate` for 12 pairs whose ground truth is `related` (e.g. cross-infrastructure causal incidents located within 150m).
   - *Likely Cause Hypothesis:* Proximity alone cannot differentiate two identical reports of the same pothole from two adjacent but distinct infrastructure defects.

3. **Convex Dilution & Threshold Conservatism (B5):**
   - *Directly Observed Evidence:* Exactly 14 duplicate pairs were misclassified into `related` (0 into `independent`).
   - *Likely Cause Hypothesis:* The fixed 60/40 combination requires both strong text ($>0.41$) and strong proximity ($>0.85$) to cross $\theta_{dup}=0.60$. Modest text similarity pulls the combined score into $[0.15, 0.60)$, causing under-classification into `related`.

4. **Zero-Error Validation Methods (HISC & B3):**
   - *Directly Observed Evidence:* Exactly 0 misclassifications observed across all 80 validation pairs.
   - *Likely Cause Hypothesis:* Combining category compatibility with spatial proximity cleanly separates duplicates from independent pairs in the standard municipal split.

### Sampling Design and Incident-Clustering Nuance

- **Challenge Set Equivalence:** In the synthetic challenge set ($N = 150$), each complaint pair belongs to a unique incident cluster (`CH_INC0001` through `CH_INC0150`), meaning cluster sizes are $M_i = 1$. Consequently, pair-level binomial intervals and incident-clustered resampling are mathematically equivalent.
- **Held-Out Test Set Caveat:** In the 400-pair development dataset, duplicate pairs share incident IDs and complaint text templates are repeated across splits (84.6% text overlap with train). The pair-level binomial intervals for test accuracy treat observations as independent trials and therefore underestimate variance due to unmodeled intra-cluster correlation.

## J. Limitations and Threats to Validity

1. **Sample Size Constraints:** The labeled dataset consists of 400 total pairs (240 train, 80 validation, 80 test). While grouped splitting prevents leakage, confidence intervals on small test splits inherently carry wider variance.
2. **Synthetic Data Separability:** The high validation performance of category-plus-distance methods indicates that synthetic template generation produces cleaner category and spatial centroids than real-world municipal complaints.
3. **Research-Provenance Chronology Findings:**
   - **Initial 5-Group Dictionary:** Introduced in commit `d5e990c` (Sep 29, 2026) in the exact same commit as `civicflow_400_labeled_complaint_pairs.csv`. Git history cannot establish which was created first.
   - **Expanded 10-Concept Dictionary:** Introduced in commit `a4446b4` (Oct 10, 2026), demonstrably postdating the 400-pair dataset and split creation.
   - **Dictionary Coupling:** While no leakage between splits occurred, potential domain coupling between dictionary terms and synthetic generation templates warrants scrutiny on out-of-distribution real complaints.
4. **Fixed Baseline Design Choices:** Negative exponential decay scale (1,000m) and B5 convex combination weights (60% text / 40% distance) were fixed by design and not optimized.
5. **Simplex Classification-Parity Audit Finding:** An audit of the T5 weight optimizer revealed a medium-severity discrepancy in the candidate evaluation heuristic (`evaluateFastWeights`) regarding Rule 5 duplicate thresholding. The selected learned weights were separately validated via the official `scorePair.js` pipeline (100% training accuracy), and all final predictions in Section E were generated strictly via the production scoring pipeline. The hand-set HISC model remains the production baseline.
6. **Held-Out Evaluation Freeze Execution:** Final held-out evaluations were executed strictly once at commit `327a4fcdbbda3f44492304871bbfbd3b8ab3c366` across test ($N = 80$) and synthetic challenge ($N = 150$) sets without post-hoc retuning.
